FAQ

Does Barengan sell concert tickets?

No. You still buy tickets on the official platforms. Barengan plans the group around it: who’s in, savings, the ticket war plan and the cost split.

Does Barengan hold our money?

No. Barengan only records savings and payments. Money moves between you and your friends, the way it does today.

What if a friend never pays me back?

Reminders go out in Barengan’s name on day 1 and day 3. After day 7, the organiser gets options: a final deadline, record a partial payment, give the spot to a backup friend at the same price, or let it go.

How do you know the ticket price before it’s announced?

We show an estimate based on similar concerts at the same venue. When the official price is out, everyone’s target updates automatically and the change is logged.

Is it free?

Planning is free for groups of up to 5. Barengan+ (Rp25,000 per group per concert) adds unlimited friends, scheduled reminders and a 50 GB album.
